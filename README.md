# Maritime Navigator

**Detailed Website Development Prompt for VOTPI Maritime Limited**

Create a premium, world-class, fully responsive corporate website for **VOTPI Maritime Limited** — a professionally managed marine transportation and logistics company operating in Nigeria’s oil & gas downstream sector. The site must look and feel like an international shipping / energy company (not a generic local business site). It should communicate credibility, financial capability, operational excellence, safety, regulatory compliance, and the ability to serve major refineries, international traders, marketers, tank farms, and energy companies.

### Brand Identity & Positioning

- **Company**: VOTPI Maritime Limited (maritime subsidiary of Pivot Energy Integrated Energy Limited / Pivot Energy & Integrated Services Limited).
- **Core message / tagline options** (use primary + secondary):
  - Primary: **VOTPI MARITIME LIMITED — Connecting Refineries to Markets by Sea**
  - Alternatives: “Moving Refined Energy. Connecting Africa.” / “Powering Africa’s Downstream Energy Movement.” / “Powering Africa’s Energy Movement by Sea”
- **Positioning**: Not just a vessel owner — an integrated marine transportation partner supporting the entire downstream petroleum value chain. Emphasis on safety, reliability, capacity, compliance, and regional reach.
- **Visual identity**:
  - Logo: Use the provided VOTPI Maritime logo (flame/drop + wave mark in orange/navy).
  - Colour palette: Deep Navy / Dark Blue + White + Metallic Grey, with restrained orange accent (from the logo).
  - Typography: Modern, clean, highly readable sans-serif (premium corporate feel).
  - Imagery style: High-resolution cinematic photography and video of modern oil/product tankers at sea, approaching terminals, loading/discharge operations, port infrastructure, marine crews, refineries, petroleum storage terminals, West African coastal waters, and navigation/bridge scenes. Dark, powerful, professional tone.
- **Tone of voice**: Confident, professional, precise, safety-focused, internationally oriented.

### Overall Design & Technical Requirements

- Premium, corporate, minimalist, modern, powerful, professional, maritime/energy-focused aesthetic.
- Fully responsive, mobile-optimised, fast-loading, SSL-secured, SEO-optimised, CMS-enabled (easy updates for fleet, news, certifications, careers, contact info).
- Compatible with modern browsers, Google Analytics + Search Console ready, protected against common threats, optimised for high-quality images/video.
- Structured data / schema markup, proper metadata, optimised headings, image alt text, internal linking, location-based SEO.
- Target SEO keywords: oil tanker company Nigeria, petroleum product transportation Nigeria, marine transportation Nigeria, oil tanker shipping Nigeria, petroleum tanker charter Nigeria, marine logistics Nigeria, product tanker West Africa, PMS/AGO transportation Nigeria, oil tanker operator Africa, etc.
- Future-ready features (phase 2): Live AIS fleet tracking, vessel availability status, client portal for voyage updates/docs/ETA.

**2. About Us**

- Company overview (professional marine transportation & logistics company in oil & gas downstream; specialises in PMS, AGO/Diesel and other refined products via dedicated product tankers; serves Nigeria + West Africa with ambition for broader African coastal markets).
- Roots / Parent: Pivot Energy & Integrated Services Limited (founded 2017, indigenous O&G company focused on trading, importation & distribution of premium petroleum products; key hubs Lagos, Calabar, Port Harcourt; one of Nigeria’s leading volume oil marketers; N100bn oversubscribed investor offer; one of only 20 approved offtakers under Dangote Refinery PMS Consortium with 300 million litres/quarter allocation).
- Mission: Provide safe, reliable, efficient and professionally managed marine transportation solutions for refined petroleum products across Nigeria and the African coastal markets.
- Vision: Become one of Africa’s most trusted and respected marine transportation companies for refined petroleum products, connecting refineries, terminals and downstream markets through world-class vessel operations and logistics.
- Core Values (present clearly):
  - Safety – We put the safety of our people, vessels, cargo and environment first.
  - Reliability – We deliver dependable marine transportation solutions and maintain operational discipline.
  - Integrity – We conduct our business with transparency, professionalism and accountability.
  - Excellence – We continuously pursue higher standards in vessel operations, technology and customer service.
  - Sustainability – We operate responsibly with a strong commitment to environmental protection and sustainable marine transportation.
- Stats / Track record: 9+ years experience, 250+ projects completed, 30+ professionals; since June 2025 facilitated clearing/agency for vessels handling 163,995 metric tons of petroleum products & crude parcels; overcoming port delays, infrastructure, security & regulatory challenges.
- “Every Litre. Every Voyage. Every Time.” messaging.
